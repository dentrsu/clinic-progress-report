Where are you now? — Verified completion percentage
▲

Measures what fraction of total duration-weighted required units have been verified by an instructor. Each requirement is weighted by est_work_duration so harder/longer requirements count more.

progress_score = round(verified_completion_pct × 100) -- numerator: only VERIFIED records, duration-weighted ver_total = SUM( (rsu_units + cda_units) × est_work_duration ) WHERE status = 'verified' -- denominator: all requirement minimums, duration-weighted req_total = SUM( (minimum_rsu + minimum_cda) × est_work_duration ) FROM requirement_list verified_completion_pct = ver_total ÷ req_total
ℹ Why duration-weighting? A Root Canal (est_work_duration = 3.0) takes 3x longer than a simple Scaling (1.0). Without weighting, completing 10 easy requirements and 10 hard ones would look the same. Duration-weighting reflects actual clinical effort.

30-Day Velocity
How fast are you moving? — Duration-weighted units verified recently
▲

Measures how many duration-weighted units (RSU + CDA) were verified in the last 4 weeks and last 8 weeks. The key date is complete_date — when the student actually finished the work — falling back to verified_at for older records.

(RSU + CDA)
×
est_work_duration
→
complete_date (primary)
→
verified_at (fallback)
→
4w & 8w windows
v_4w = SUM( (rsu_units + cda_units) × est_work_duration ) WHERE status = 'verified' AND COALESCE(complete_date, verified_at) >= NOW() - 4 weeks v_8w = SUM( (rsu_units + cda_units) × est_work_duration ) WHERE status = 'verified' AND COALESCE(complete_date, verified_at) >= NOW() - 8 weeks
ℹ Why two windows? 4-week is sensitive (what's happening now). 8-week is stable (smooths out bursts). The forecast uses whichever is more favorable. Only verified records count — pending and completed are excluded from velocity.

3
Estimated Completion Date
When will you finish? — Linear projection from current velocity
▲

The Oracle asks: "If you keep completing work at your current speed, when will you reach 100%?"

-- Step 1: Best monthly velocity (weighted units/month) monthly_velocity = GREATEST( v_8w / 2.0, -- 8-week avg (stable) v_4w, -- 4-week rate (current) 0 -- floor at zero ) -- Step 2: Remaining work (absolute weighted units) remaining = req_total - ver_total -- Step 3: Months remaining months_remaining = CEIL( remaining / monthly_velocity ) -- Step 4: Project the date forecast_date = date_trunc('month', TODAY + months_remaining)
ℹ The forecast uses absolute weighted units (req_total − ver_total), not percentages. This means harder requirements (higher duration) naturally require more months to complete.

Example Walkthrough — Step 1 of 4

Student A from our earlier examples: ver_total = 58.75, req_total = 520.0.

4-week weighted velocity: 56.25. 8-week weighted velocity: 65.75.

monthly_velocity = GREATEST(65.75/2, 56.25, 0)
  = GREATEST(32.88, 56.25, 0) = 56.25

The 4-week velocity wins because the recent month had more verifications.

Example Walkthrough — Step 2 of 4

Calculate remaining work in absolute weighted units:

remaining = req_total - ver_total
  = 520.0 - 58.75 = 461.25 weighted units

This accounts for the difficulty of remaining requirements through their duration weights.

Example Walkthrough — Step 3 of 4

How many months at this pace?

months_remaining = CEIL(461.25 / 56.25)
  = CEIL(8.2) = 9 months

Example Walkthrough — Step 4 of 4

Project from April 2026:

forecast = April 2026 + 9 months = January 2027

Displayed as 1/1/2570 (Buddhist Era).

When to Trust the Oracle

High Trust
  • ✅ Active student, consistent completions
  • ✅ Near graduation deadline
  • ✅ Risk score trends over time
Use Caution
  • ⚠ First weeks with 1–2 records
  • ⚠ Returning after a long pause
  • ⚠ Requirements with default est_work_duration (1.0)
